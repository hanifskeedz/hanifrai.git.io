✦ Our Story ✦

Made with Love,
Baked with Heart

Trifa's Cake N Sweet is a homemade bakery — a warm, cozy space where every bite tells a story. Founded by Hanif Rai, with a passion for bringing joy through handcrafted sweets.

Every recipe is made from scratch using wholesome ingredients, baked fresh to order, and packaged with care. Whether you're treating yourself or surprising someone special, we've got just the thing.

Have questions or want to place an order? Chat with us on WhatsApp — we'd love to hear from you.
